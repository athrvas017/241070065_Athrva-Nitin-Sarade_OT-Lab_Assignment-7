# Track A Prompt

Implement continuous optimization methods in pure NumPy from scratch:
1. First-order: GD, SGD with noise, Mini-batch, Momentum, NAG, AdaGrad, RMSProp, Adam.
2. Second-order: Newton's method and Gauss-Newton on nonlinear least squares.
3. Compare on convex quadratic, Rosenbrock valley, double-well, and saddle surface ($x^2 - y^2$).
4. 2-layer MLP with manual analytical backpropagation and numerical gradient check ($< 10^{-6}$).
5. Finite-difference Hessian-Vector Product, diagonal Fisher approximation, and low-rank curvature.
6. Generate convergence curves, step-size sensitivity, noise robustness plots, and verify Newton 1-step quadratic convergence.
